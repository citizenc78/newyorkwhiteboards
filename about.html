Who we are

Most suppliers sell whiteboards alongside a thousand other office products. We come at it the other way: writing surfaces are the whole business, and have been for over half a century. Our group spans both sides of the Atlantic — from the North American manufacturer whose founder built the first whiteboard in 1967, to the continent's largest online collection of visual communication products, to a UK specialist that has equipped thousands of schools. New York Whiteboards brings all of that to the five boroughs.

Made properly, backed properly

The manufacturing side of the group designs and builds its boards in-house in North America, from North American-sourced materials, under ISO 9001 quality and ISO 14001 environmental management — with GREENGUARD Children & Schools certification across its products. That's why you'll find the group's surfaces everywhere from home offices to Fortune 500 boardrooms, hospitals, universities and government buildings — and why some of our ranges carry warranties as long as fifty years.

What that experience buys you

It means we know the difference a surface makes: why melamine ghosts and porcelain doesn't, when glass earns its premium, which frame survives a school corridor and which is fine in a boardroom. It means relationships with the category's best manufacturers — Quartet, MasterVision, Ghent, Luxor, Aarco among them — alongside the group's own ranges, and the buying power to price sharply on multiples.

How we work

We spec for the room, not the invoice. Our advice starts with how the space is actually used: where a standard board does the job, that's what we recommend, and where a harder-working surface is worth the investment, we explain exactly why. Every quote is a fixed price, lead times are stated accurately, installation is arranged through approved professional fitters, and every surface we supply is backed by its full manufacturer warranty.

Who we supply

Offices from two desks to two towers, schools and universities, healthcare and labs, creative studios and everyone in between — across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island, with trade and education pricing where it applies.

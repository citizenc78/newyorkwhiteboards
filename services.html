Services

We measure, deliver and arrange installation — one point of contact from first call to first meeting.

Site surveys & advice

Not sure what fits, or what the building will allow? We survey the space, check wall construction and access routes, and recommend the right board for how the room is actually used.

Delivery

Stock boards ship from our warehouse within two working days. Our own drivers handle Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island, including timed deliveries, certificates of insurance and freight-elevator bookings for managed buildings.

Installation

We arrange professional installation through our approved local fitters — boards fixed level, secure and to manufacturer spec, with evenings and weekends available for occupied offices and schools.

Aftercare

Replacement trays, pen sets and cleaning kits, plus resurfacing and re-hanging when spaces get reconfigured. If a board ever ghosts or stains within the warranty period, we replace it.

Not sure what fits your space?

Send us the wall size or a photo and we'll recommend the right board, with a fixed price.

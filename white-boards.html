Custom whiteboards, built for how you work

When the standard sizes don't fit the wall — or the workflow — we build the board to order. Every custom board starts from a premium writing surface chosen for your environment, magnetic or non-magnetic, then takes the size, color and frame style the space calls for, with practical add-ons where they earn their keep: marker trays, cork map rails, even printed graphics for planners, schedules and branded layouts.

Size & frame

Any dimension up to full-wall, in a range of frame styles and colors — or frameless, edge to edge.

Surface

Painted steel, porcelain or glass, specified to how hard the room works — an upgrade that outlasts any worn melamine board.

Add-ons & print

Trays, map rails, and printed elements — grids, calendars, process layouts — baked permanently into the surface.

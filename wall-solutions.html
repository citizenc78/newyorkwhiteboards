The whole wall is the workspace

A framed board fills a corner of a wall; a writable wall removes the frame entirely. Ideas run uninterrupted across project rooms and design studios, sprint walls hold a week of thinking, and training rooms get a surface that shrugs off class after class. From glare-free finishes that double as projection surfaces to non-porous panels specified for clinical spaces, there’s a wall build-up here for every environment.

Specified by environment

Collaboration spaces

Continuous full-height surfaces for project rooms, studios and war rooms — built for design reviews, workshops and work that develops across the whole wall rather than one board at a time.

Education & training

Heavy-rotation rooms need a finish that doesn’t ghost or stain. These surfaces keep their legibility through years of daily erasing, from the first class of the morning to the last.

Healthcare & labs

Non-porous, cleanable panels with antimicrobial finishes for infection-sensitive zones — with custom printed graphics available for care protocols and staff communication.

Whiteboard walls

Steel-based writing surfaces that go on over the wall you already have — the fast route to a full-height magnetic writing zone.

EVS Skins

A steel-backed writable skin that converts an existing wall into a full-height magnetic writing surface. It installs cleanly over finished construction — no structural work — which makes it the go-to for renovations, retrofits and tenants who can’t touch the fabric of the building.

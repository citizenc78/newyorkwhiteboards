Services

From an enormous standard range to fully bespoke boards built to order — we help you choose it, build it to your spec, and get it to you. One point of contact from first idea to finished wall.

Bespoke boards, built to order

This is the heart of what we do. When the standard catalogue doesn't fit the wall, the workflow or the brand, we manufacture the board to your specification — any size up to a full wall, in painted steel, porcelain or glass, framed in your choice of style and color or fully frameless. Add marker trays, map rails, and printed elements — grids, calendars, planners, process layouts or full branded graphics, baked permanently into the writing surface. Magnetic or non-magnetic, projection-friendly or high-gloss: the choice is genuinely huge, and every combination starts from a premium surface built to perform for years.

Specification & advice

The range being huge is only useful if you land on the right combination. Tell us how the room is used and we'll narrow it fast — surface samples where it helps, honest guidance on where porcelain or glass earns its premium, and a check of the space itself so what's ordered is what fits.

Delivery

Stock boards ship within two working days, and bespoke builds come with realistic, stated lead times. We deliver across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island — timed deliveries, certificates of insurance and freight-elevator bookings for managed buildings included.

Installation & aftercare

Where you want the board on the wall as well as through the door, we arrange professional installation via approved local fitters. And the relationship doesn't end at handover: replacement trays, pen sets and cleaning kits, re-hanging when spaces get reconfigured, and warranty support on every surface we supply.

Not sure what fits your space?

Send us the wall size or a photo and we'll recommend the right board, with a fixed price.

Boards are all we do

New York Whiteboards is part of an international group of specialist writing-surface businesses — companies that have spent decades doing one thing: putting the right board on the right wall.

Who we are

Most suppliers sell whiteboards alongside a thousand other office products. We come at it the other way: writing surfaces are the whole business. Behind this site sits a group that supplies and manufactures boards on both sides of the Atlantic — for corporate headquarters, universities, school districts, hospitals and studios — and New York Whiteboards brings that depth of stock, spec knowledge and supplier relationships to the five boroughs.

What that experience buys you

It means we know the difference a surface makes: why melamine ghosts and porcelain doesn't, when glass earns its premium, which frame survives a school corridor and which is fine in a boardroom. It means relationships with the category's best manufacturers — Quartet, MasterVision, Ghent, Luxor, Aarco among them — and the buying power to price sharply on multiples. And it means we've seen enough walls to spec yours right the first time.

How we work

Honestly, mostly. If a $97 board does the job, we won't sell you a glass wall; if the room genuinely needs porcelain, we'll say so and show you why. We quote fixed prices, keep lead times realistic, arrange professional installation through approved fitters, and stand behind every surface we supply — up to fifty years on some ranges.

Who we supply

Offices from two desks to two towers, schools and universities, healthcare and labs, creative studios and everyone in between — across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island, with trade and education pricing where it applies.

Put the experience to work

Tell us about the space and we'll spec it like we've specced a thousand before — because we have.
